Train it yourself

Train a universal computer of 8,170 parameters on your own computer, in this page, by gradient descent: it learns the steps of a universal Turing machine from random machines. Then test it, exactly, on twelve programs it never saw: seven small machines, three cellular automata, and the busy beavers. The training runs on your GPU (WebGPU), or on your processor's cores if there is none.

What it learns about each token

Each token's learned embedding, 8 numbers, shown by its three main directions of variation, moving as it trains. Two tokens are marked: go_aS:I and go_bS:I, "write 0, stay" and "write 1, stay" on a marked 1. With too little data the network misses a step for the first that it has seen for the second, because to it they are unrelated tokens. Watch whether they end up close.
