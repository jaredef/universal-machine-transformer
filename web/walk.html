Where the 23,844 parameters are

Almost all of them are the two lookup tables at the ends, one row per token. The computing in between is 832 numbers.

The forward pass is Network.prototype.forward in web/core.js, the same function the decoder runs; the scene is web/inside-scene.js. The windows it never saw: web/build_inside.js.
