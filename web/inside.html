One forward pass, inside 23,844 parameters

The network that runs the sieve of Eratosthenes does one thing, billions of times: it reads three tokens of the universal machine's transcript and writes what comes next. Here is one of those passes, taken live from the sieve and walked through layer by layer. Every weight is drawn, and every number the pass computes, as it computes it.
